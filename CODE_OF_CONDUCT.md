# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

FMWarriors is a private community of Claris / FileMaker professionals. We are committed to maintaining a respectful, collaborative, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or language.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional in all interactions.
- Give and receive constructive feedback graciously.
- Share knowledge openly and generously.
- Respect differing opinions and approaches.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Spam or off-topic self-promotion without context.
- Sharing private information of others without consent.

## Enforcement

This community is self-regulated. Members are expected to use common sense and good judgment. Persistent violations may result in removal from the group.

## Reporting

If you witness a violation, raise it directly with a group moderator or bring it to the attention of the community.
